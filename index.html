Music

I'm a film composer and music producer. Since 2023 I've scored short films that range from action to drama.

Most of my inspiration comes from video game soundtracks like Mario Kart, Pokémon and Terraria, which is probably why I keep reaching for music that feels nostalgic and cinematic.

Before I left for Hong Kong, I wrote that I wanted to write the music for my own Wong Kar Wai life.2

Community

I used to be incredibly shy.3 Now I'm also president of QBA, the club for quantitative biology majors, and I mentor younger students.

我

About

I grew up in Cupertino keeping fish, which is how I got into biology.

I spent spring 2026 on exchange at CUHK, in a dorm that looked out over Tolo Harbour. That's the mountain at the top of this page.4 My family is from Beijing and Changchun.

于是仲春令月，时和气清；原隰郁茂，百草滋荣。

"In the fine month of mid-spring, the season is mild and the air clear." 张衡，《归田赋》

My name and my brother's, 时 and 和, come from this line by Zhang Heng, the Han-dynasty astronomer, inventor and writer. He worked across astronomy, engineering and literature, which is roughly the energy I'm going for. [CONFIRM SOURCE WITH PARENTS]

The fish at the bottom are for the paludarium on my desk and the pond at CUHK.5

I spent spring 2026 on exchange at CUHK, living in S.H. Ho College, a residential college where everyone eats dinner together three nights a week. I took Cantonese and Urban China alongside BioMEMS and bionanotechnology, and joined the college run club. Coming in, I told myself I'd make the most of it, keep a good journal and write it all up.
